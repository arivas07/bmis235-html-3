Trip 2: Siena

Nestled in the rolling hills south of Florence, Siena feels like stepping straight into the Middle Ages. Its winding, car-free brick alleys open up into the famous Piazza del Campo, home to the historic Palio horse race.

While in Siena, I want to visit the striped black-and-white Siena Cathedral, try traditional ricciarelli almond cookies from a local bakery, and watch the sunset over the Tuscan vineyards.
