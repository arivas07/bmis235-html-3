Trip 1: Pisa

Just an hour west of Florence by direct regional train, Pisa is the easiest half-day trip in Tuscany. Walking across the Arno River through the historic student quarter leads straight to the sprawling green lawn of the Piazza dei Miracoli.

Beyond taking the classic photo holding up the Leaning Tower, I plan to climb the 294 spiral steps to the top for panoramic views of the city and explore the massive marble Duomo right next door.
