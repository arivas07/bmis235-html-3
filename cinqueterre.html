Trip 3: Cinque Terre

For a coastal escape, Cinque Terre is a string of five pastel-colored fishing villages perched dramatically on the Ligurian cliffs. Each town, Monterosso, Vernazza, Corniglia, Manarola, and Riomaggiore are connected by a quick local train.

My plan is to hike the scenic trail between Vernazza and Monterosso, grab fresh focaccia and seafood cones by the harbor, and swim in the Mediterranean Sea before catching the evening train back to Florence.
